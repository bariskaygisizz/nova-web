1. What NOVA is

NOVA helps you prepare for and practise difficult workplace conversations: you describe a situation, prepare, role-play it with an AI-played (or, when live AI is off, script-played) counterpart, and get feedback against a fixed, visible rubric. Scenarios in the library are fictional. Scores are a practice aid calculated by the app from yes/no criteria; they are not an assessment of you or anyone else. NOVA also includes an AI assistant chat, a day planner, a Smart Vault for notes and ideas, short wellness and nutrition ideas, games (NOVA QUEST, NOVA Arcade, a Gaming Hub list), NOVA Impact and NOVA Translate.
