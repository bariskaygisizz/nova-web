Privacy Policy

NOVA, the conversation rehearsal and coaching app for new managers, with the NOVA AI assistant, day planner, Smart Vault, wellness, nutrition, games and translation (iPhone). Last updated: 11 October 2026.

This policy describes what the iPhone app described on this page does with your data. The Android edition of NOVA is a different product with its own policy.

The short version

  • Your conversations, prep notes, role-play transcripts, scores, reflections, follow-ups, tasks, goals, Smart Vault items, chat history and game progress are stored on your device only. NOVA has no accounts and keeps no copy of them on a server.
  • NOVA asks for your permission before anything is sent to live AI. If you allow it, the text you type in a step that uses AI is sent to our server, which forwards it to a third-party AI provider (Groq, with OpenRouter as a backup) and returns the answer. If you say no, nothing is sent: clearly labelled on-device scripts, suggestions and a keyword check are used instead.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • The app never asks for your camera, contacts, location or health data. It asks for the microphone and speech recognition only if you tap the microphone to talk to NOVA AI; photos or files you add to the Smart Vault are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.
  • You can delete everything NOVA keeps on your device at any time: Settings → Delete my data.

What stays on your device

Conversations you create (title, the other person's name or stand-in and role, what happened, the outcome you want, what worries you), prep notes, role-play transcripts, scores and the criteria behind them, reflections and follow-ups; tasks, goals, Smart Vault items and the photos or files you add to them, NOVA AI chat history, wellness and nutrition entries, your gaming list, NOVA QUEST and Arcade progress and NOVA Impact entries; your settings (live AI on or off, language); how many live-AI requests you have made today (for the daily limit); a random install identifier; and simple step counters that are used only to build the optional “Share feedback” report (counts and your note — never your conversation text). Like other apps, NOVA's data may be included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your device settings.

When live AI is used (only after you allow it)

NOVA uses live AI for the other person's lines in a role-play, scoring and feedback after a rehearsal, drafting a scenario from your description, and reflection questions after a real conversation. It also uses live AI in the NOVA AI chat, to plan a day or goal, to suggest wellness breaks and meal ideas, to summarise and tag Smart Vault items, and in NOVA Translate.

What is sent: the text you typed in that step, plus the details needed to answer (for a rehearsal: the title, the other person's name or stand-in and role, what happened, the outcome you want, what worries you, the reaction style you chose and earlier turns of the same rehearsal; for the other tools: your recent chat messages, the task, Vault text or text to translate, and any focus areas or daily priority you have set), and the app language. Photos and files are never sent. With each request the app also sends a random install identifier and whether you have NOVA Pro, which our server uses only for daily limits. Please use a first name or a stand-in, and do not enter anyone's full name or sensitive personal information.

How it is handled: our server (hosted on Vercel) checks the request, applies the daily limit and forwards the text to the AI provider; the request comes from our server, so the provider does not receive your IP address or install identifier from us. Our server does not store or log what you write. The AI providers (Groq, and OpenRouter and the model companies it routes to) process the text to produce a reply and may keep it for a limited time under their own terms and privacy policies. As with any web host, our hosting provider may log technical request data such as IP address and time.

Your choice: turn live AI off at any time in Settings → Allow live AI. Replies are labelled whenever they come from an on-device script, suggestion or keyword check instead of live AI.

Voice (optional)

If you tap the microphone in NOVA AI, iOS asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition (which may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy); NOVA receives only the text, never stores recordings, and treats the text like a typed message. Replies read aloud use your iPhone's built-in voices. You can decline the permission and keep typing.

Subscriptions

NOVA Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether NOVA Pro is active: it receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase receipt. The anonymous ID is shown as “Support ID” at the bottom of Settings so support can find your subscription record if you contact us.

What this version does not do

This version of the iPhone app includes no advertising and does not send analytics or push-notification data to any third party. It does not ask for access to your camera, contacts, location or photo library (photos and files you add to the Smart Vault come from the system picker, which gives NOVA only the item you choose), and it asks for the microphone only when you tap the microphone.

Website and contact form

If you use the support form, we receive your name, the optional email address you give us, the topic and your message. It is delivered to the developer by email and as phone notifications (through Resend, ntfy and CallMeBot/WhatsApp) and used only to answer you. Please do not include passwords or payment details. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and do not use cookies or trackers of their own.

Service providers

RevenueCat (subscription status), Apple (App Store payments), Groq and OpenRouter and the model companies they route to (AI text, only after you allow it), Vercel (hosting of our server), GitHub Pages (this website), and, for the support form only, Resend, ntfy and CallMeBot. Each processes data only to provide its service to NOVA. Some of these providers are located outside your country, including in the United States.

Retention and deletion

  • On your device: kept until you tap Settings → Delete my data (which removes everything NOVA stores, including tasks, Vault items, chat history and game progress) or delete the app.
  • On our server: AI requests are not stored. Daily-limit counters are kept in memory only and reset daily.
  • Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat, and support messages you send us: to ask us to delete or correct them, contact us and include the Support ID from Settings if you have a subscription.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK or California law) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because NOVA keeps your conversations only on your device, you can already see and delete them yourself. For anything held by us or our providers, use the support form.

Children

NOVA is intended for people aged 13 and over (the coaching content is for adults who manage or are about to manage people). It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not professional advice

NOVA gives general communication coaching and practice, and general wellness and nutrition ideas. It is not HR, legal, medical, dietary or mental-health advice.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its date, and ask for your permission again where the change affects what is sent to AI.

Contact

Questions or data requests: use the support form.
