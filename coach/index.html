For people new to managing

Rehearse the hard conversation before you have it.

Giving critical feedback. Addressing repeated lateness. Saying no to your director. NOVA lets you prepare, role-play it, and see exactly what to change — then try again.

How it works

  1. Describe it. Who it is with, what happened, the outcome you want and what worries you. Or pick a scenario from the library.
  2. Prepare. Your goal, facts, likely reaction and what you must not do, in one short summary.
  3. Role-play. The other person reacts in the style you choose — defensive, confused, emotional, dismissive, argumentative and more.
  4. See why you scored what you did. Clarity, Specificity, Tone and Clear ask are calculated from visible yes/no criteria. Tap any skill to see which were met, which were not, and the evidence from your own words.
  5. Try again and compare. Attempt 1 versus attempt 2, skill by skill.
  6. Reflect and follow up. After the real conversation, note what happened and set one follow-up.

Also in NOVA

The coaching screens are the heart of the app. Beside them you also get the NOVA tools:

  • NOVA AI — ask, plan and capture ideas in a chat, by typing or by voice (optional).
  • Today — tasks and goals with AI-planned steps.
  • Smart Vault — notes, links and ideas, with AI summaries and tags.
  • Wellness and Nutrition — short movement breaks and flexible meal ideas (general information, not medical advice).
  • Games — NOVA QUEST, NOVA Arcade and a Gaming Hub bucket list, and NOVA Impact for small good deeds.
  • NOVA Translate — text into 20+ languages.

NOVA is available in English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian and Russian.

Honest about AI and your data

  • Conversations, scores, reflections, tasks, Vault items, chat history and game progress stay on your device. There are no accounts.
  • Text is sent to AI (Groq, with OpenRouter as a backup) only for the step you run — a role-play turn, scoring, a scenario draft, reflection questions, a NOVA AI message, a plan, a meal or wellness idea, a Vault summary or a translation — and only after you allow it. Without it, clearly labelled on-device scripts, suggestions and a keyword check are used instead.
  • Coaching scores are calculated by the app from a fixed rubric, never made up by a model.

Free and Pro

The core features are free. NOVA Pro is an optional subscription with more daily live-AI requests, advanced manager scenarios (conflict, performance, credit-taking, burnout, compensation), an unlimited Vault and goals, longer wellness plans and NOVA QUEST cosmetics.

NOVA gives general communication coaching. It is not HR, legal, medical or mental-health advice.
