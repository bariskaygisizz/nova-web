Help with NOVA, the conversation rehearsal and coaching app for new managers. Start with the quick answers; if they don't solve it, send us a message below.

Quick answers

How do I turn live AI off?
Open Settings → When AI is contacted and switch off Allow live AI. The other person is then played by an on-device script and scoring uses an on-device keyword check, both clearly labelled.

How do I delete my data?
Open Settings → Privacy → Delete my data. This removes your conversations, scores, reflections and follow-ups from your device. NOVA keeps no copy on a server.

The other person's lines look scripted. Why?
Live AI is off, unavailable at the moment, or you have used today's live-AI requests. The app tells you on screen when this happens.

How do I manage, cancel or restore NOVA Pro?
Manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings → NOVA Pro in the app and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

An AI reply was inappropriate or wrong.
Tell us using the form below (topic: Feedback) and describe what happened, without including anyone's personal details. We use these reports to improve the prompts and safeguards.
