Delete your NOVA ID

In the app (recommended)

Open NOVA → Profile → NOVA ID → Delete NOVA ID. This signs out every device and immediately deletes your NOVA ID, its device list, security log, recovery code record and encrypted backup from our server.

Data kept only on your phone (tasks, Vault, games and settings) is removed with Profile → Delete my data, or by uninstalling NOVA.

Without the app (lost or reset phone)

Enter the recovery code from your NOVA recovery kit. Your code never leaves this page: your browser turns it into a one-way value, which is all our server receives.

What is deleted, and what isn't

  • Deleted immediately: your NOVA ID, all device public keys, device names and activity times, the security log, the recovery-code record and your encrypted backup.
  • Kept for 30 days: an anonymous deletion record (date and platform only — no NOVA ID, no device) to investigate abuse. It is then removed automatically.
  • Not affected: subscriptions are managed by your app store — cancel them there. Anonymous purchase records held by RevenueCat can be deleted on request via our contact form.

Without your devices or your recovery code we can't tell which NOVA ID is yours — there is no email or password on file — so we can't delete it for you. Backups are end-to-end encrypted, so nobody, including us, can read them.
