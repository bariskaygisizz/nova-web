Privacy Policy

DRAFT — prepared by the developer for launch and not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Summary

  • Your tasks, Smart Vault items, reflections, chats, games list and NOVA QUEST progress are stored on your device.
  • NOVA AI asks for your permission before anything is sent to live AI. With your permission, the text you submit is sent to our server and forwarded to a third-party AI provider (OpenRouter and the model providers it routes to) to generate a response. We do not sell it and do not use it for advertising. You can turn live AI off in Profile → Live NOVA AI; NOVA then answers with on-device suggestions.
  • We never ask for permission to access your contacts, location, photo library or camera. NOVA asks for the microphone only when you tap the microphone to talk to NOVA, and listens only while the voice orb is on screen.
  • If you choose to add a photo or file to the Smart Vault (or share one to NOVA from another app), the system picker gives NOVA only that item. It is copied into NOVA's private storage on your device and is never uploaded.
  • A custom app background (NOVA Pro) — a photo or video you pick, or one taken from a website link you paste — is saved on your device only.
  • NOVA ID (optional) has no email, password or social login. Your device key stays in your phone's secure hardware; our server stores only its public key, your devices and an end-to-end encrypted backup that only your recovery code can open.
  • You can delete all on-device NOVA data at any time: Profile → Delete my data.

Data we process

  • AI requests. The prompt text you submit, a little context you chose in NOVA (focus areas, today's priority) and a random installation identifier (for rate limiting). Photos and files are never sent. Requests are not linked to your name or email. Our server does not log request contents. NOVA may use free AI models whose providers can keep submitted text and use it to improve their models, so please don't enter sensitive personal information (such as health, financial or login details) in NOVA AI.
  • Voice (optional). When you talk to NOVA, your speech is turned into text by your device's built-in speech service (on Android usually Google's, on iPhone Apple's), which may process the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy. NOVA receives only the text, never stores recordings, and treats that text like a typed message. Replies read aloud use your device's text-to-speech engine.
  • Translations. Text you ask NOVA Translate to translate is sent to our server and an AI provider in the same way as an AI request.
  • Background from a link (optional). If you paste a website link to use as your background, your device downloads that page and its preview image or video directly from that website; the website sees a normal request from your device.
  • NOVA ID (optional). If you create one, we store: a random NOVA ID (e.g. NOVA-7K2MQ-9XD4R), each device's public key, device name and platform (e.g. “Galaxy S23 · Android 14”), when devices were added and last active, a security log of the last 50 events (such as “device added” or “recovery code used”), a one-way hash of a value derived from your recovery code, and your backup ciphertext. Your backup is encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your recovery code, which never leaves your devices, so we cannot read it. We never receive passwords, private keys or your recovery code, and signing requests are single-use. Profile → NOVA ID → Delete NOVA ID (or, without your phone, the web deletion page with your recovery code) deletes all of this immediately; we keep only an anonymous deletion record (date and platform, no ID) for 30 days to investigate abuse.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple, Samsung, Google or Stripe and managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts to confirm your NOVA Pro status.
  • Notifications (optional). If you opt in, OneSignal receives a push token and coarse engagement tags (for example “completed today's priority: yes/no”, streak length, chosen focus area). No task text or personal content is sent.
  • Product analytics & attribution. Event names such as “task_completed” or “paywall_view” with non-personal properties (counts, feature names) may be sent to Layers (attribution) and PostHog (product analytics, EU region) to measure which features and campaigns help people. No free-text content, no session recordings.
  • Ads (Android, optional). NOVA QUEST can show an optional rewarded ad served by Google AdMob; RevenueCat records ad impressions and revenue. Google may use the advertising ID as described in its policies. Ads are never required to use NOVA.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, we receive your name, the optional email address you give us, the topic and your message. It is delivered to the developer by email (via Resend) and as phone notifications (via ntfy and CallMeBot/WhatsApp), and used only to answer you. Don't include passwords or payment details.

Service providers

RevenueCat (subscriptions), OneSignal (push notifications), Layers (analytics & attribution), PostHog (product analytics), Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads, Android), AI text generation through OpenRouter, Vercel AI Gateway or Groq and the model providers they route to (for example Google, OpenAI, Meta, NVIDIA), Resend, ntfy and CallMeBot (contact-form delivery), Upstash (NOVA ID database, encrypted backups), and our hosting providers (Vercel for our server, GitHub Pages for this website). Each processes data only to provide its service to NOVA.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off in Profile or your system settings.
  • Turn personalization off in Profile — suggestions then ignore your activity.
  • Delete all on-device data in Profile → Delete my data. To delete data held by service providers, contact us and include your RevenueCat app user ID (shown to support on request).

Children

NOVA is not directed to children under 13.

Health & wellness

Wellness, nutrition and career content is general information, not medical, dietary, legal or mental-health advice.
